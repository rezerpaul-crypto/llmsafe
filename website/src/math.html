Every number has a rule.

How the app counts.

No black box. These are the rules behind the figures in Prop Firm OS, in plain words.

Taken from the logic of the current build.

Win

Journal

net ≥ $1

A trade counts as a win when it closes one dollar or more in profit.

Daily loss room

Risk & Finance

limit + today's net

Worked out per account from the trades you logged today. It never shows less than zero.

Win rate

Dashboard, Analytics

wins ÷ (wins + losses)

Break-even trades are left out, so scratching a trade does not move your win rate in either direction.

Break even

Journal

$0 ≤ net < $1

Listed on its own. It is neither a win nor a loss.

Near limit

Risk & Finance

75% of the limit used

The risk monitor flags an account once today's loss reaches three quarters of its daily limit.

Evaluation progress

Dashboard

profit so far ÷ target

Shown as a percentage, capped at 100%, next to the dollars still to go.

Loss

Journal

net < $0

Anything below zero.

Limit reached

Risk & Finance

today's loss ≥ limit

The account shows “Limit reached” instead of a remaining amount. A limit of 0 switches the check off.

Edge by setup

Analytics

net P&L per setup

Setups are ranked by net result in the range you pick. Trades without a setup are grouped as Unclassified.

Week and month

Homepage

Monday · the 1st

The week total starts on Monday, the month total on the first day of the month.

Calendar times

Homepage

your local time

Release times come from the Forex Factory export and are shown in your timezone. Holidays are hidden.

Recap

Coach

written on your device

The coach compares the day's wins and losses and writes one or two plain sentences. No trades are sent to an AI service.
