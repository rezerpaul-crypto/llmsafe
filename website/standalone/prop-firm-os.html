An account is rarely lost on the last trade.

It goes a few trades earlier, when the daily limit stops being a number you can see.

You size up to get back to even, and the loss you were allowed runs out before the session does.

A rule you have to remember is a rule you break on a bad day.

So Prop Firm OS keeps the number on the screen.

A funded account is a set of rules.

The daily limit does not move.

One green day is not an evaluation.

Write the trade down. Then take the next one.

Rules are numbers: a daily loss limit, a profit target, an account size. Prop Firm OS keeps all three in front of you, for every account you trade.

What memory is good at

You know your strategy and how the session felt. What memory does badly is arithmetic under pressure: adding up three trades across two accounts while the next setup forms.

What the desk does

It adds up the trades you logged today on each account, compares the total with the daily loss limit you set and shows what is left. At 75% of the limit it warns. At the limit it says so.

Every number has a rule.

How the app counts.

No black box. These are the rules behind the figures in Prop Firm OS, in plain words.

Taken from the logic of the current build.

Win

Journal

net ≥ $1

A trade counts as a win when it closes one dollar or more in profit.

Daily loss room

Risk & Finance

limit + today's net

Worked out per account from the trades you logged today. It never shows less than zero.

Win rate

Dashboard, Analytics

wins ÷ (wins + losses)

Break-even trades are left out, so scratching a trade does not move your win rate in either direction.

Break even

Journal

$0 ≤ net < $1

Listed on its own. It is neither a win nor a loss.

Near limit

Risk & Finance

75% of the limit used

The risk monitor flags an account once today's loss reaches three quarters of its daily limit.

Evaluation progress

Dashboard

profit so far ÷ target

Shown as a percentage, capped at 100%, next to the dollars still to go.

Loss

Journal

net < $0

Anything below zero.

Limit reached

Risk & Finance

today's loss ≥ limit

The account shows “Limit reached” instead of a remaining amount. A limit of 0 switches the check off.

Edge by setup

Analytics

net P&L per setup

Setups are ranked by net result in the range you pick. Trades without a setup are grouped as Unclassified.

Week and month

Homepage

Monday · the 1st

The week total starts on Monday, the month total on the first day of the month.

Calendar times

Homepage

your local time

Release times come from the Forex Factory export and are shown in your timezone. Holidays are hidden.

Recap

Coach

written on your device

The coach compares the day's wins and losses and writes one or two plain sentences. No trades are sent to an AI service.
